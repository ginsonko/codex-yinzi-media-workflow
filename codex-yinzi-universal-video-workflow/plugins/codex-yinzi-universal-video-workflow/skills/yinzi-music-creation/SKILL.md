# 银子歌曲创作

Turn the user's story and mood into an actual musical result. Distinguish a **sung song** (DiffSinger score or YuE2), **instrumental background music** (MusicGen or the score route's band), and **spoken narration / dialogue** (TTS). TTS speaks; it never sings. Pitch/time editing is useful for a deliberately spoken remix; it is not a substitute for singing.

## Pick the route first

- **One-call tool `create_music` (`omni_media`)** — call `create_music({ kind: 'song'|'rap'|'instrumental'|'theme', story, mood, style, seconds, lyrics?, voice?, route?: 'auto'|'score'|'yue2'|'musicgen' })` first whenever the user, 漫剧总监, 流水线 or 超级导演 needs a finished song, rap, theme/ending song or custom instrumental without hand-editing individual notes. With `route='auto'`, vocals run the local score route (¥0, outputs master WAV/MP3, instrumental stem, LRC, 1080p lyric video and `.ustx`) and pure instrumentals run commercial-friendly SoundFont arrangement. If it returns `status='pending'` with `job`, wait with `wait_media` or `wait_for`; if `status='need_prepare'`, tell the user the reported download size, estimated time and licence notice before retrying with `allow_download=true`. Always state the returned `license` boundary (DiffSinger community vocoder is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 noncommercial; YuE2/MusicGen are CC-BY-NC-4.0 noncommercial).
- **精确作曲（score route, manual script control）** — choose `scripts/score_song.py` directly when you need syllable-by-syllable custom melody/harmony edits, spoken-to-sung cepstral voice matching, or phrase-level re-renders: self-praise idol songs with call-and-response, 相声/说唱 where every punchline lands on a beat, ad jingles, children's songs, multi-character duets, songs for a beat-synced MV, or when the user wants it free and editable phrase by phrase. You write lyrics + melody + harmony + rhythm per syllable; DiffSinger voicebanks sing it locally; the band is rendered from the chord sheet; the script mixes to -14 LUFS and checks itself with speech recognition, beat tracking and loudness. Read [score route](references/score-route.md) and run `scripts/score_setup.py --inspect`, then `scripts/score_song.py` (sample with `--demo` first), `scripts/lyric_video.py` for the lyric/beat video, `scripts/score_qa.py` to re-check.
- **AI whole-song generation** — choose it when the user gives a mood or story and wants a full-sounding produced song with little control over individual notes, or wants a realistic band/human timbre. Continue with the sections below.
- They combine: AI sketch → transcribe to MIDI → refine in the score route; or score-route vocals over an AI instrumental.

Score-route rules: rap and crosstalk-rap are DiffSinger notes too (`rap`/`pocket` tags bend pitch along each syllable's Mandarin tone in a narrow range); TTS may only voice un-timed spoken lines (报幕, 「去你的吧」), or a clearly labelled per-character forced-aligned TTS comparison; label any un-timed TTS as a placeholder and keep it out of finished songs. Voicebanks, vocoders and soundfonts are large and are downloaded on first use into the data directory, never bundled. Before downloading, tell the user the size and the licences in [score licences](references/score-licenses.md): the DiffSinger community vocoder is CC BY-NC-SA (non-commercial), Alemon and Ria have no stated commercial permission, 绮萱 allows commercial use with credit. Warn the user whenever they intend to publish or sell.

Use the sibling [media workflow](../codex-yinzi-universal-video/SKILL.md) for task progress, module discovery and local-job APIs. Keep current user choices and authorization. The chosen local route or an already authorized retry needs no additional approval.

Before selecting models or writing a full song, follow the shared [creative clarification](../codex-yinzi-universal-video/references/creative-clarification.md) flow. Establish the intended feeling, story/message, sung versus instrumental result, and any consequential preferences for voice, language or length. Infer from existing material and ask only what changes the direction; do not turn these into a required checklist. In guided mode, offer understandable listening or descriptive contrasts when the user cannot name a genre. In AI-directed mode, choose and explain a coherent direction without optional preference questions. Save confirmed choices separately from assumptions, audition a representative section when helpful, and diagnose vague dissatisfaction in the vocals, words, emotion or arrangement before regenerating the song. Preserve accepted audio.

## Choose a usable route

YuE2 and MusicGen are optional, on-demand components. Do not download their weights during ordinary workflow installation, browsing a capability list or preparing an unrelated task. When a requested task actually needs song/music generation, compare routes during planning: inspect the actual OS, GPU model, total/free VRAM, available RAM, CUDA/driver where applicable, and free disk at environment/model/output paths. Explain the useful local route and its preparation cost, then download only the selected components within the user's authorization. Do not treat a published minimum or a module being listed as evidence that this machine is ready.

Read repository `docs/LOCAL-SONG-YUE2.md` for portable setup/inspect commands. Prepare only the chosen model in an isolated environment; preserve music/voice configuration and reuse verified weights. A short CPU MusicGen run may be an option when time permits, but benchmark it before promising practical throughput.

YuE2 through WanGP offers INT8, CPU offload and tiled VAE. About 4.5 GB is a low-memory route to evaluate, not a guarantee for every 4.5 GB card. Prototype evidence used an 8 GB NVIDIA GPU; length, concurrent processes and runtime affect the peak. Start with a representative section and inspect the receipt. Do not silently substitute an unverified CPU YuE2 implementation. MusicGen CPU makes short instrumental music, not vocals.

If hardware is unsuitable, explain the measured constraint and offer an online song service. Check current official availability, lyrics control, API access, price, output rights and data handling. Do not label a third-party Suno wrapper as its official API or assume Yinzi API has a music endpoint. A recommendation alone does not authorize uploading or payment. YuE2 and bundled MusicGen/F5 weights have noncommercial licensing; software licensing does not override model rights.

Useful starting points are [Suno](https://suno.com/) with its [official help/tutorials](https://help.suno.com/), and [Udio](https://www.udio.com/) with its [official help](https://help.udio.com/). These are service and learning links, not promises of an available public API or a particular free tier. If the user prioritizes low cost, offer existing authorized music, spoken narration or a TTS rhythm placeholder as appropriate; clearly label a spoken draft and do not present it as a lower-quality version of actual sung music. Avoid configuring or downloading a local singing model for a task that only needs dialogue.

## Make the music

1. Preserve story beats; design genre, voice roles, emotional progression, hook and sections. Write performable lyrics with rhyme, stress, silence and varying density. A memorable hook can return with a changed response or consequence. Avoid a universal characters-per-bar formula.
2. Inspect `local.audio.neural-song` and use UTF-8 lyrics, style and the desired length. Use `score_file` only with an actual compatible ABC score. Save seed, parameters, revision and input identity. A score guides structure; it does not guarantee identical bilingual timing or singers.
3. Test a representative hook/transition before expanding an uncertain direction. Choose a suitable sample length rather than a fixed 9/30-second requirement. Preserve accepted audio and change only remaining parts unless revision is requested.
4. Review actual singing: core lines, pronunciation, omissions, emotion, vocal balance and transitions. A lively backing track does not compensate for flat or incorrect vocals. ASR flags possible mismatches but is not ground truth. A decodable WAV proves execution, not performance quality. Read [vocal production](references/vocal-production.md).
5. Deliver original WAV, a listening file and lyrics/section times. Keep gain and mixes separable. Attenuating accompaniment should not also fade vocals. Mixed outputs do not imply perfect source stems.

For bilingual versions, translate meaning and rewrite stress for the phrase; audition both independently. A reused score is not proof of matched timing. Keep each accepted master separate and use its actual phrase boundaries.

For an MV, build the beat/lyric timeline from the chosen audio and read [performance and compositing](../codex-yinzi-universal-video/references/music-mv-story-and-compositing.md). Carry the approved sample's craft through the whole film; do not substitute repetitive motion or a weaker visual route to fill the remaining duration. For expressive lettering, language variants and release cuts, read [authored typography and bilingual delivery](../codex-yinzi-universal-video/references/authored-typography-and-bilingual-delivery.md). The techniques adapt to the user's genre and style; they do not require anime, chibi characters or constant effects.

Expose a stable, playable milestone through the workflow's current artifact/progress contracts as soon as it exists, with its actual project directory, version and review state. A listening draft or render candidate is useful before final delivery. Register it after the file is complete and decodable; report what is ready and what remains. A chat message or a file sitting on disk alone does not make it discoverable in the workbench.

## Recover without deadlocks

Keep failed logs and outputs. Import failure, GPU exhaustion, invalid audio and artistic rejection have different remedies. Correct the cause and create a new local attempt; reuse valid weights and completed sections. For a cloud call with unknown acceptance, reconcile the preserved request/task identity before deciding another submission. Do not silently replay it. An explicitly authorized retry within existing scope/budget creates a fresh attempt while retaining the old task and unknown charge. A failed download normally resumes the existing asset, not generation. Uncertainty or a stale status must not create a permanent retry ban.
